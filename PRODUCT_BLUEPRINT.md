# Product Blueprint: Dish-Level Food Curator App

## 1. Product Vision
Create a trusted food discovery app that helps people decide what specific dish to order based on recommendations from real food lovers, restaurant staff, and frequent customers.

## 2. Problem Statement
People often know which restaurant to visit but still struggle with what to order. Existing review platforms are overloaded with generic restaurant reviews, fake reviews, and too many opinions that do not match personal taste.

## 3. Core Insight
A trusted human recommendation, such as a waitress who personally tasted menu items, is more useful than hundreds of generic online reviews.

## 4. Differentiation
This app focuses on:
- Dish-level recommendations
- Curator identity and credibility
- Personally tasted dishes
- Taste preference matching
- Images and short practical notes

It should not compete directly as a generic restaurant review app.

## 5. Target Users

### Food Curators
People who enjoy tasting and recommending dishes.
Examples:
- Restaurant staff
- Foodies
- Frequent customers
- Local cuisine specialists
- Vegetarian food experts
- Spicy food lovers

### Food Seekers
People who want quick answers to:
- What should I order here?
- What is the best vegetarian dish nearby?
- What is spicy but not too oily?
- What dish is actually worth trying?

## 6. MVP Value Proposition
Find the best dish to order, recommended by people who actually tasted it.

## 7. Key Features

### MVP Features
- User authentication
- Curator profile
- Add restaurant
- Add dish recommendation
- Upload dish image
- Add taste tags
- Add short review notes
- Mark personally tasted
- Home feed
- Basic filtering

### Later Features
- Follow curators
- Save dishes
- Curator trust score
- Restaurant staff verified badge
- AI food assistant
- Personalized recommendations
- Community collections
- Dish comparison

## 8. Trust Model
Trust is central to the product.

Early trust signals:
- Personally tasted checkbox
- Curator profile
- Dish count
- Likes/saves
- Photos
- Clear taste notes

Future trust signals:
- Verified restaurant staff
- Repeat engagement
- Community feedback
- Report/flag system
- Curator ranking by category

## 9. MVP Launch Strategy
Start with one local market only.

Recommended initial goal:
- 1 city
- 20 trusted curators
- 50-100 dish recommendations
- 10-20 restaurants

Measure whether users use the app to decide what to order.

## 10. Success Metrics
- Number of dish recommendations posted
- Number of active curators
- Number of dish searches
- Number of saves/likes
- Repeat usage
- User feedback: Did this help you decide what to order?

## 11. Risks
- Cold start problem
- Low content quality
- Fake recommendations
- Becoming another Yelp clone
- Too much AI before product validation
- Restaurant bias or paid manipulation

## 12. Product Rule
Do not scale features until dish-level recommendations prove useful.
